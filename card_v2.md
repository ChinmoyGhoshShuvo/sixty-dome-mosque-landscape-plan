**Title:** Landscape Plan for the Sixty Dome Mosque, Bagerhat

**Summary:** For a landscape planning studio, our team surveyed a UNESCO World Heritage mosque complex and mapped its land use, sun path and wind. We identified eight gaps in visitor facilities, from ablution space and toilets to parking, lighting and security. We then designed and modelled in 3D a heritage-sensitive solution for each one.
